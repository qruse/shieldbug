# Shieldbug icon — round 2

Read `BRIEF.md` first (same goals and rules). Your round-1 files are `icon.svg`, `icon-small.svg`, `icon-mono.svg`; a render of them is `preview_v1.png`.

The owner likes the direction (a smiling shield bug). Fix these points:

1. **It reads as a leaf, not a bug.** The vertical centre line looks like a leaf vein. Remove it. Instead, split the back into **two wing covers (elytra)** like a ladybug or real shield bug: a clear seam from a small head/pronotum down to the tail, with the two halves meeting at the bottom point of the shield. Add a **distinct head** at the top (a small rounded segment above the shell) so the silhouette says "insect" before it says "shield".
2. **Small sizes lose the face.** In `icon-small.svg`, keep only the essentials: shield silhouette, head, antennae, and **two large eyes**. Drop the mouth and legs if they turn to mush at 16 px. Eyes must still be visible at 16 px.
3. **The one-colour version looks like a cat or ghost.** Make `icon-mono.svg` follow the new silhouette (head + shell seam + antennae) so it reads as a bug.
4. Keep it friendly and simple. No extra decoration, no gradients that blur at small size.

Write the new files as `icon-v2.svg`, `icon-small-v2.svg`, `icon-mono-v2.svg` in this folder (keep the round-1 files). Update `NOTES.md` with a short round-2 paragraph. Do not touch anything outside this folder.
