# Shieldbug icon — round 3

Read `BRIEF.md` (goals and rules) and `BRIEF_v2.md`. Your round-2 files are `icon-v2.svg`, `icon-small-v2.svg`, `icon-mono-v2.svg`. `preview_v2.png` shows round 2 (top two rows) next to round 1 (bottom two rows).

The owner likes round 2: it now clearly reads as a bug, and it survives at 16 px. Keep the two wing covers with the centre seam and the shield-shaped body.

**One serious problem: it looks too much like the Android robot logo** (green half-round head, two straight antennae, two white dot eyes), especially the one-colour version. A security tool must not be mistaken for another company's mark. Fix it like this:

1. **Head**: replace the half-round dome with a **wide, flat, slightly triangular head** (the real shield-bug head shape: a broad pronotum with a small blunt head in front). Its outline must not be a semicircle.
2. **Antennae**: **curved and slightly segmented-looking** (a gentle bend or a small tip bulb), not two straight sticks.
3. **Insect cues**: add a few **short legs** peeking out from the sides of the shell and/or **two or three small spots** on the wing covers. Keep it simple enough for 32 px; in `icon-small-v3.svg` you may drop legs and spots.
4. **Eyes**: keep them friendly and large enough for small sizes, but not two identical white dots in a dome. For example, eyes set at the sides of the head, or eyes with a small pupil.
5. **Colour**: move the green away from Android's yellow-green. Use a deeper, slightly teal/emerald green for the shell (for example around `#0f9d6b`–`#12805c`) with a dark forest outline. Keep good contrast on white and near-black.

Deliver `icon-v3.svg`, `icon-small-v3.svg`, `icon-mono-v3.svg` in this folder (keep earlier files). Add a short round-3 paragraph to `NOTES.md`. Do not touch anything outside this folder.
