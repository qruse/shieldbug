# Shieldbug icon

A smiling shield bug turns the protective shield into a small, approachable guardian. Brand green anchors the shell, with a deep forest outline for definition and a pale keyline to keep the full-colour icons distinct on dark backgrounds. The small and one-colour versions retain the same face and shield silhouette with fewer details.

Round 2 gives the bug a distinct rounded head and two wing covers that meet at the shield's point. The small version enlarges the eyes and removes the mouth, while the one-colour version uses open space for the eyes and shell seam.

Round 3 replaces the dome with a broad, angular head and adds curved, tipped antennae to give the bug a more distinctive silhouette. Short legs and two shell spots add insect cues to the full-colour icon; the small version omits them for clarity. The shell now uses emerald greens with a dark forest outline and a pale keyline for contrast on dark backgrounds.

## Round 4 (final)

A friendly guardian beetle combines an angular, expressive head with a broad shield-shaped shell and a clean central seam. Curved antennae and short legs give it character, while the simplified and single-colour versions retain the same protective silhouette at smaller sizes. Palette: ink and pupils #0f3a2c; left shell #1fae7a; right shell #169468; head #2fbf8a; eye white #ffffff; dark-background keyline #eafff5.
